# Plain language

## Terms

- Keep a technical term the reader needs, and explain it at first use.
- Spell out an abbreviation the first time, unless every reader knows it.
- No metaphors: say what you mean.
- Describe what the reader will see or has to do, not the internal name of the thing.

## Sentences

- Split a sentence over about 25 words.
- Active voice: name who acts.
- Present tense, unless accuracy needs another.
- Verbs as verbs: "we analyze the data", not "we conduct an analysis of the data".
- Avoid semicolons; readers misread them.
