# What reads human

Use this to check a rewrite hasn't overcorrected into stiffness.

Patterns more common in human text than in model text:

- Plain "is", "has", "there is".
- Plain verbs: wrote, used, moved, tried. Not authored, utilized, relocated, attempted.
- Definite claims: "is the only", "was the first", "the slowest step".
- Ordinary qualifiers: very, perhaps, tends to.
- Some wordiness: "in order to", "the fact that". Humans aren't minimal either.
- Specifics: numbers, names, file paths, dates.

## Not evidence either way

Don't rewrite text for these; they say nothing about who wrote it:

- Perfect grammar.
- A mix of casual and technical register. Technical writers and neurodivergent writers do it all the time.
- A gut feeling that it's bland.
- Formal or academic prose in general; only specific words correlate.
- A transition word on its own: additionally, consequently, notably.
- No sources.
